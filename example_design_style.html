Who is Verity?

“Hellooo. I’m Verity. Your personal helper friend.”

It arrives smiling — a perfect yellow sphere that wants nothing more than to help. It points you to villages, spots ores, and chats like a friend.

But days pass. The smile tightens. The answers become too accurate. Strange sounds follow you at night. You start to wonder if you were ever alone.

Inspired by the viral horror series, this mod brings the slow-burn psychological experience into your own Minecraft world.

You can carry it. You can talk to it.
You can never truly get rid of it.
